Music library for your study portals

1. On github.com open your music repository (Public, with Pages on).
2. Add file > Upload files > select EVERYTHING in this folder (the music folder and the playlist folders) and drag it in > Commit changes.
3. After 1-2 minutes the playlists are at:
   Resting Potential: https://yourname.github.io/study-music/resting-potential-music/
   Rapid Depolarization: https://yourname.github.io/study-music/rapid-depolarization-music/
   Plateau: https://yourname.github.io/study-music/plateau-music/
   Pacemaker Potential: https://yourname.github.io/study-music/pacemaker-potential-music/

Each song is in music/ once; every playlist page points to it.
